Let it remind you.

let it remind you is a calm, private iPhone app for the dates that matter only once: a free trial that starts charging, the last day to return an order, the end of a warranty, something borrowed or lent, a document that expires, a recurring maintenance task.

Save a reminder

Type what to remember, swipe sideways on the category name to change the category, tap one of the date buttons or choose Custom, then save. The row next to the save button sets the alert day, the time and whether it repeats.

Notifications

You get one local notification on the day and at the time you choose. Press and hold it to mark the reminder done or to be reminded again tomorrow. Notifications can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Notifications not arriving?

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Remind You, and make sure a Focus mode is not silencing it. In the app, Settings → Notifications must be on. Opening the app once reschedules every alert.

Repeating reminders

Repeat every week, month or year, or choose Custom for your own rhythm, such as every 10 days. When you complete a repeating reminder, it moves to its next date.

Managing reminders

The folder button shows your reminders. Search, filter by category, tap to edit, swipe right when done or left to delete. Both can be undone for a few seconds.

Backup and restore

Settings → Export reminders creates a checked backup file that you save wherever you like. Restore it on any iPhone through Settings → Restore backup. Restoring replaces the reminders on that iPhone.

Coming soon

Settings → Capture shows features on the way. Natural language input and Smart typing can already be tried for an hour, as often as you like. Nothing is sold in this version.

Your data

Your reminders stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server of our own.
