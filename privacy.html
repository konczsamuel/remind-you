Privacy policy

Private by default.

Last updated: November 10, 2026. This policy covers the iPhone app let it remind you, made by Koncz Samuel.

In short

The app does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not send your reminders or settings to any server.

What the app stores on your iPhone

Reminder names, categories, dates, alert times, repeat settings and your app settings are stored only on your iPhone. The app keeps two local copies to protect against damaged data. If you choose to keep an unfinished reminder, its text is stored on your iPhone for up to one day.

Notifications

Alerts are iOS local notifications. They are scheduled and delivered by your iPhone; no notification service of ours is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Backups

Export creates a backup file only when you ask for it. You decide where it is saved or shared through the iOS share sheet, and the file stays under your control. Restoring reads a file you pick and does not send it anywhere.

Feature previews

Features you try for an hour, such as Natural language input and Smart typing, run entirely on your iPhone. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no data is shared with anyone. Apple’s own App Store and iOS terms apply to downloading and running the app.

Deleting your data

Delete reminders in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone. Backup files you exported are deleted wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: konczsamuel@protonmail.com.
